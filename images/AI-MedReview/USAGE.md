# AI-MedReview — Listening

Two listening forms surround a central patient voice. The identity is designed for patient-feedback analysis.

## Files
Use logo-horizontal-color.svg for headers and documents; logo-stacked-color.svg for centred layouts; symbol-color.svg for compact spaces; app-icon.svg for the application. PNG files have transparent backgrounds except the app icon and presentation preview. White files are intended for dark backgrounds. SVG wordmarks contain outlined Arial Bold letterforms and do not require font installation. No font files are included.

## Palette
Navy #082B50; teal #13838A; dark-background accent #72D4D0; white #FFFFFF.

## Use
Keep clear space at least equal to the central dot diameter around the mark. Use the symbol at 24 px or larger; favicon is supplied for smaller browser contexts. Keep the horizontal logo at least 220 px wide. Preserve proportions and use the supplied colour variants. Avoid effects, rotation, stretching or placing the mark on busy images.

This is an independent product identity; the artwork contains no NHS branding. Trademark clearance has not been performed.
